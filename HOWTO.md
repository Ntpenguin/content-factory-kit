# Content factory: how to run things

Moved out of `STATUS.md` on 2026-11-16. Commands run from this folder.

- Dashboard (the whole factory on one page): double-click `dashboard.cmd`, or `python -m factory.dashboard`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:8790`. Laptop only; it stops when its window closes. Tabs: Today (what waits on Nate, counts, credits), Batches (every ad and video with Approve, Reject and a comment, saved straight to `decisions.json` in the batch folder), Brands (facts, never-claim lines, offers, look), Run (the steps below as buttons, one at a time, with a Stop button; refused 16:35 to 17:10 CT Sunday to Thursday, and a run still going at 16:45 is stopped), Library (every document). It never spends and never posts: voice lines and pictures still need a session. Check: `python -m factory.dashboard --selfcheck`. A new step gets a button by adding one line to `ACTIONS` in `factory/dashboard.py`.
- Brand check: `python -m factory.brand --selfcheck` and `python -m factory.brand builtwithbaron` (from this folder).
- Hook sheet: edit `runs/<brand>/<batch>/hooks.json`, then `python -m factory.hooks <brand> <batch>`. It writes `hook-sheet.md` and `matrix.csv` only if every hook passes. Checks of the checks: `python -m factory.hooks --selfcheck`, `python -m factory.claims`.
- Write new scripts: the first button on the dashboard's Make tab (pick the brand, who it's for and 5, 10 or 20 hooks), or `python -m factory.write <brand> "<customer>" <count>`. A background Claude run (Opus, on the Claude plan; no generation credits) reads the knowledge base, the brand's facts and the earlier batches, and drafts `hooks.json`, `statics.json`, `videos.json` and `notes.md` in a new folder `runs/<brand>/<date>-scripts[-n]`. It has five file tools and may write those files and `evidence.md` only. The copy checks then run; what fails goes back to it up to 3 times. "DONE" means every line passes and `hook-sheet.md` is written; "NOT DONE" leaves drafts that are not ready. About 4 minutes for 5 hooks. The brand needs one earlier batch that passes `python -m factory.write --check <brand> <batch>` (it is the worked example). Nothing is rendered: render the statics next; videos need voice lines from a session. Checks: `python -m factory.write --selfcheck`.

- Static ads: edit `runs/<brand>/<batch>/statics.json`, then `python -m factory.statics <brand> <batch>` (gates the copy, renders 3 sizes into `pieces/`, gates the images, writes `gate.json`; about 90 seconds for 10 ads). First time on a machine: `npm ci` inside `remotion/`.
- Review page: `python -m factory.review <brand> <batch>` writes `review.html` in the batch folder. It is a plain file: opened from disk it keeps decisions in the browser and Export saves them. Opened through the dashboard (the "Review page" link on a batch) it saves each decision straight to `decisions.json`.
- Rendered images and videos (`pieces/`) are not in git (17 MB for 30 images); the spec, `gate.json` and `review.html` are. Re-run the lane to rebuild them.
- Checks of the gate: `python -m factory.gate`.
- Videos: edit `runs/<brand>/<batch>/videos.json`, then `python -m factory.video <brand> <batch>` (about 5 minutes for 5 videos in 2 sizes; counts as heavy work). It names any voice line that is missing (`media/vo/<hash>.mp3`); a session makes those on Higgsfield (`generate_audio_batch`, model `seed_audio`, the voice in the spec; the result may come back as wav, so convert with `ffmpeg -i in.wav -codec:a libmp3lame -q:a 2 <hash>.mp3`), two jobs at a time because more are refused, each priced and logged in `spend.md` first. Check of the lane: `python -m factory.video --selfcheck`. It needs `ffmpeg` on PATH and the `faster-whisper` package with model `base.en` (already in `C:\Python314`; nothing was installed).
- Faceless explainer: in `videos.json` give the piece `"lane": "faceless"` and put `"image": "media/img/<name>.png"` on every beat but the end card (rows are optional, 0 to 4; 10 to 25 s). A session makes the pictures on Higgsfield (`generate_image_batch`, model `gpt_image_2_6`, quality medium, resolution 1k, aspect 9:16, about 0.5 credit each, four jobs at a time because more are refused), each priced and logged in `spend.md` first, with no people, no text and no logos in the prompt; keep the prompts and job ids in `media/img/prompts.json`. Then run the video lane as usual. The example is `runs/builtwithbaron/2026-11-16-faceless/`.
- Pictures behind a punch video (since 2026-11-18): put `"image": "media/img/<name>.png"` on any beat of a `"lane": "punch"` piece except the end card and a recorded-call beat (`"audio"`); it is optional per beat. The rows land over the picture, and a picture held over 3.2 s cuts once to a closer crop. Make the pictures as for the faceless lane. Ask for daylight with the top quarter and bottom third darker, because the headline and captions sit there. The example is `runs/builtwithbaron/2026-11-18-pictures/` (batch 1's four voice-over videos; its voice files were copied from batch 1, so the voice cost nothing).
- Board video (since 2026-11-19; the look of the reel Nate sent: a dark board of numbered cards, no person, no photos): in `videos.json` give the piece `"lane": "board"` and a `"series"` name of at most 4 words; every beat between the hook and the end card needs a `"part"` (the card's title), a `"widget"` (the drawing in the card: `list`, `phone`, `steps`, `thread`, `flow`, `check` or `site`) and 1 to 3 rows; no `image`; 10 to 45 s. Each beat is one card, and the card colours start from the hook's number (six colours in turn, so hooks six apart start on the same one). Only the voice lines cost credits. The example is `runs/builtwithbaron/2026-11-19-organic-info/` (five videos, each with a different first drawing); its `posts.py` builds the page of posts with captions.
- Fact-first board opening (since 2026-11-19; worked example `runs/builtwithbaron/2026-11-19-fact-first/`): the hook gets a `"stat"` (the big figure, counted up on screen) and a `"source"` (named on screen), and cites an `evidence.md` line graded `[stat]`, used word for word. The first beat plays a 5 s realistic clip under it. Make the clip on Higgsfield in two steps, each priced first: a still (`generate_image_batch`, `gpt_image_2_6`, medium, 1k, 9:16, 0.5 cr; no faces, no readable text), then `generate_video_batch` with `kling3_0`, std, 5 s, 9:16, `"sound": "off"` (a string: `false` falls back to sound on at 10 cr), starting from that still (7.5 cr). Two or three jobs at a time; more come back 429. Voice lines on `elevenlabs_v4` take `{"model": "elevenlabs_v4", "prompt": line, "dialogue": [{"text": line, "voice_type": "preset", "voice_id": "f1373f24-3b96-433f-9a68-e595810ef608" (Kevin)}]}` (0.23 cr short, 0.46 long). `jobs_wait` takes `jobs: [{index, job_id}]` and at most 15 s. Look at every clip before use: AI phones show made-up names and text. Blur a small patch with ffmpeg (`[0]split[a][b];[b]crop=W:H:X:Y,boxblur=14:3[c];[a][c]overlay=X:Y`); `delogo` leaves streaks. Keep the original as `<name>-raw.mp4`.
- One-line video (one continuous shot, one line of light that ends as the logo; rules in `knowledge/editing-rules.md` "One-line style"): edit `runs/<brand>/<run>/line.json` (worked example: `runs/builtwithbaron/2026-11-18-line/line.json`), then `python -m factory.line <brand> <run>` (about 3 minutes for two sizes; heavy work). Voice lines come from the batch named in `"lines_from"`; a missing one stops the run before rendering. The last stop draws Built with Baron's mark; another brand needs its own in `remotion/src/Line.tsx`. Check: `python -m factory.line --selfcheck`.
- Sound in videos: a batch picks its music bed with `"music"` in `videos.json` (`""` for none); the files and where they came from are in `sounds/` and `sounds/SOURCES.md`. The levels are at the top of `factory/video.py` (`VOICE_DB`, `BED`, `BED_END`, `SFX`).
- Look pass, after every video render: `python -m factory.look <brand> <batch>` makes a six-frame sheet per video (`pieces/<piece>/look-<size>.png`) and lists the videos nobody has looked at. Look at each sheet, then `python -m factory.look <brand> <batch> <piece> "<note, signed>"`, then rebuild the review page. A re-render wipes the note on purpose. Check: `python -m factory.look --selfcheck`.

- Phone clip: put the clip in `runs/<brand>/<batch>/media/raw/`, then `python -m factory.footage <brand> <batch> <path to clip>`. It prints what it dropped and writes `media/clips/<name>-cut.mp4` and `<name>-cut.json`. Read the "say" text in the json against the clip, fix misheard words, replace "FILL IN" with the fact ids, paste the "beat" into `videos.json` as the first beat of a piece with `"lane": "footage"` and `{"show": "end"}` as the last, then run the video lane. Put a wrongly dropped phrase back with `--keep <its number>`. If a noisy clip comes back uncut, raise `QUIET` in `factory/footage.py` (-30dB, -25dB). The worked example is `runs/builtwithbaron/footage-standin/videos.json`. Graphics over the clip: the picture steps 10% closer and back at each join the cutter made (automatic, at most once a second; `PUNCH_IN` in `factory/video.py`, 1.0 turns it off). Pop-up cards: add `"rows"` to the clip beat, each `[kind, text, fact ids, cue]`, where the cue is a few words from the clip's line; the card lands as they are said, one at a time above the captions. Up to 8, at least 1.2 s apart, in the order said, and each goes through the claims check. Raw clips and cuts are not in git (the stand-in's are). Check of the cutter: `python -m factory.footage --selfcheck`.

- Results and scoreboard: put a results CSV in `runs/<brand>/<batch>/results/` (columns are listed at the top of `factory/learn.py`; the piece id from the review page must start the ad's name), save the review page's exported `decisions.json` in the batch folder, then `python -m factory.learn <brand> <batch>` writes `scoreboard.md`. The sample: `python -m factory.learn builtwithbaron 2026-11-16-batch1 runs/builtwithbaron/2026-11-16-batch1/results/SAMPLE-results.csv` writes `SAMPLE-scoreboard.md`. Check: `python -m factory.learn --selfcheck`.

- Read pass (claims a rule cannot judge): `python -m factory.read <brand> <batch>` writes `read-pending.json` (the rubric and every line nobody has read). Hand it to a fresh subagent or to Nate, never to whoever wrote the copy; save the answers with `python -m factory.read <brand> <batch> <verdicts.json>` into `read.json`, then rebuild the review page. A verdict is keyed by the line and its facts' wording, so editing either makes the line unread again. The review page lists at the top the lines the reader flagged, the lines not read yet and the lines citing no fact. Check: `python -m factory.read --selfcheck`.
- Frame scan: the video lane checks every video at 6 frames a second for anything outside the safe zone (skipping clip and picture scenes, which fill the frame on purpose). To scan videos already on disk without a re-render: `python -m checks.scan <brand> <batch>`.
- A body that must carry a label (M1 is the demo call) says so in `hooks.json`: `"bodies": {"M1": {..., "label": "demo_call"}}`. The gate fails a piece on that body without the label.
- Packages: `requirements.txt` (plus ffmpeg on PATH and `npm ci` in `remotion/`).
- Checks of the checks (failure cases that must fail): `python -m checks.neg_statics`, `checks.neg_video`, `checks.neg_footage`, `checks.neg_learn`. Run them after changing a lane or the gate.
