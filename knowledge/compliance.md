# Compliance: what copy and pieces may not do

Not legal advice. Nate's rules [S14] come first; the rest is desk research [S10, S12]. Lines marked **(gate)** are checked automatically; a piece that fails stays failed and is listed.

## Nate's rules [S14]

1. **Claims come only from the brand's `facts.md`.** A piece lists the fact ids it relies on. **(gate)**
2. **Nothing outward-facing without Nate's yes:** no posting, sending, uploading, publishing or launching.
3. **No AI person claims to be a customer.** **(gate: first-person customer phrases are blocked)**
4. **No prices, guarantees, financing or shipping terms** unless they are in `facts.md`. **(gate)**
5. **AI label:** off on paid AI ads, except an ad with a realistic AI person shown to New York audiences (California from 2027), where the law needs it. The gate adds the on-screen line to any piece marked as having a realistic AI person. **(gate)**

## FTC [S10]

- **Fake reviews and testimonials (16 CFR 465):** no AI or hired person saying or implying "I bought this, I used it, it worked for me". No invented reviews, star ratings, customer counts or results. Penalties up to $53,088 per knowing violation (the note's figure).
- **Substantiation:** hold the evidence for every performance claim before the ad runs. A statistic names its source on screen [S12]. **(gate: every number must appear in a cited fact)**
- **Results and income promises about AI:** the FTC's "Operation AI Comply" (September 2024) went after AI products sold with earnings and results claims [S12]. No "never miss a call", "double your leads". **(gate)**
- **Demos are real and labelled:** an AI demo shows only what the product really does; a scripted demo says so ("Demo call · AI voice", "Example texts") [S10, S12]. **(gate: a piece with demo audio or example texts must carry the label)**
- **No fake scarcity:** no invented countdowns, "only 3 left", or limits that are not real. **(gate)**
- A label does not fix a deceptive ad.

## Meta [S10, S11, S12]

- No copy that asserts or implies a personal attribute: "Struggling?", "Losing money?", "Can't afford staff?", "Behind the times?". Talk about the business, not the viewer. **(gate)**
- No before-and-after results made or altered by AI.
- Meta's AI creative enhancements off (the advertiser answers for Meta's edits since 2026-10-30).
- Declare a special ad category for financial, housing or employment ads. (Not these brands today.)

## AI people and voices [S10]

- New York requires a conspicuous disclosure for a synthetic performer in an ad since 2026-06-09; California from 2027-01-01.
- An AI presenter speaks as a narrator in the third person ("here is how it works"), never as a customer or a named real person.
- No real person's face or voice without written permission; generated characters must not resemble a real person. Nate's own face and voice are his to use.
- Keep a record per piece: tool, prompt, whether an AI person appears, labels, the facts cited. The factory writes this beside every piece.

## Other brands and proof [S12]

- Other brands are named only as facts, never borrowed: no logos, no "partner of Google or OpenAI", no "as seen on". **(gate)**
- Rival ads give angles, not lines. No naming or attacking a competitor.
- Work for another business is stated as fact and by its real type: Fast Fix Work is a moving company; say what Built with Baron can set up for a contractor, never what it has. **(gate)**
- No real customer data on screen: dashboards and call logs in ads use made-up data and say so.

## Music and sound [S10]

- Paid ads use only music with a commercial license (or music the factory generates itself). No trending or consumer-library songs under an ad.

## What the gate cannot judge (a person must)

- Whether a picture misleads (an AI scene that shows something the product does not do).
- Whether a line implies more than its cited fact says. The review page shows each claim beside its fact for this reason.
- Whether an AI person looks "realistic" for the New York rule: the piece's spec declares it, and the reviewer confirms.
