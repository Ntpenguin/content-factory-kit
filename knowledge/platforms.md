# Platforms

Nothing is posted or launched by the factory. Organic posts wait for Nate's approval; paid ads are launched by Nate. This file is what the pieces must fit.

## Sizes the factory renders

| Name | Pixels | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1x1 | 1080x1080 | Feed (Facebook, Instagram) |
| 4x5 | 1080x1350 | Feed, the tallest feed size |
| 9x16 | 1080x1920 | Reels, Stories, TikTok, Shorts |

**Safe zone used by the gate (9x16):** keep text and logos out of the top 14% (270 px) and the bottom 20% (384 px), and 6% (65 px) in from each side. These are this factory's working margins, chosen to clear the app buttons and caption areas on Reels, TikTok and Shorts. They were not read from each platform's current spec page tonight; re-check before a paid launch. For 1x1 and 4x5 the margin is 5% on every side.

## Meta (Facebook and Instagram) [S11, S12]

- Vertical 9:16 video and images; Reels is more than half of Instagram's ad slots (strength B/C in the source).
- Primary text up to 125 characters and headline up to 40 characters before it is cut off [S12]. **(checked by the gate)**
- Small budget: 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 3 to 6 distinct concepts; add new concepts to the live ad set [S11, strength C/D].
- Objective for a local service: Leads; calls for urgent trades, a higher-intent form or the website for considered services [S11].
- Location is the hard control; interests are suggestions since 2025 [S11]. The hook does the picking: name the trade.
- **Turn Meta's AI creative enhancements off** for text, music and generated content: since 2026-10-30 the advertiser is responsible for Meta's edits [S11].
- No personal-attribute wording ("Struggling?", "Do you have…") [S10, S12].
- Native-looking Reels creative ran about 5% cheaper per result; everyday presenters beat celebrities 1.7x [platform studies via S9].
- Fatigue: refresh when frequency passes 2.5 or cost per result climbs (practitioner rule, strength D) [S11].

## TikTok [S9, S11]

- TikTok's own guide: hook in 0 to 3 s, product within 5 s, 15 to 40 s long; "DIY, not overly polished".
- AI content label: the "AI-generated content" toggle on ads and the creator label on organic posts [S10].
- Lead ads: Instant Form, Maximum Delivery, at least $50 per campaign and $20 per ad group [S11].
- Posting through Zernio and TikTok stays off until Nate says otherwise.

## YouTube Shorts [S8]

- The viral set: median 16 s; 82% are 30 s or shorter; most use music with little or no speech.
- Set the AI-use setting in YouTube Studio for AI-made scenes [S10].

## Instagram organic [S9]

- Instagram will not recommend watermarked, blurry or visibly recycled Reels: upload the clean file, never a TikTok download.
- Trending audio is for organic posts added in the app; paid ads use licensed music only.

## Measuring [S3, S9, S11]

- Hook rate (3-second views over impressions) is read with hold rate. Neither has a threshold on file.
- Views are not sales: judge ads on cost per lead and on how many leads fit the ICP.
- Platform-reported gains are ceilings, not expectations [S11].
