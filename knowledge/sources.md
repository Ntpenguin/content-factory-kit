# Knowledge base: where every entry comes from

Each entry in `knowledge/` ends with a source id in brackets, like `[S3]`. Grades:

- **watched in full**: the whole caption track was read (and frames viewed where noted). Still the speaker's claims.
- **secondhand summary**: no link or transcript on file; only someone's summary of it.
- **dataset**: counted from collected data on disk.
- **research notes**: Claude's desk research from named public sources, graded inside the note itself.
- **project doc**: a document in one of Nate's projects.
- **article, read in full**: a written article read from start to end. Still the author's claims.

Numbers from videos are the speaker's claims and are written that way. None was checked against an ad account. Nothing here comes from Nate's own ad results, because no ad has run yet.
